# SCAN Framework – Customer Friction & Pain Point Discovery

A lightweight framework to consistently uncover, validate, and document customer pain points so you can turn real friction into actionable automation opportunities.

---

## What SCAN Stands For

### **S — Survey (or Seek)**
Gather direct feedback and evidence of friction.

**Where to gather signals**
- Live conversations (calls, discovery meetings)
- Surveys and short questionnaires
- Customer support tickets / call transcripts
- CRM notes and lost-deal reasons
- Website chat logs and inbound inquiries

**Great questions to ask**
- “What part of your day feels harder than it should?”
- “Where do leads or customers fall through the cracks?”
- “What’s the most repetitive thing your team does every day?”
- “What breaks when you’re short-staffed or after hours?”

**Outputs**
- Raw notes, quotes, examples
- Specific moments of frustration (“last week we…”) and frequency (“daily/weekly”)

---

### **C — Categorize (or Check)**
Organize what you found into clear pain-point types.

**Core categories**
- **Financial** — lost revenue, high costs, wasted spend, billing friction
- **Productivity** — time sinks, interruptions, manual follow-ups, slow handoffs
- **Process** — confusing steps, duplicated work, broken workflows, onboarding friction
- **Support** — missed calls, slow responses, poor customer experience, lack of help

**How to categorize quickly**
- Ask: “If we solved this, what improves first?”
  - Money → Financial
  - Time → Productivity
  - Steps/flow → Process
  - Customer satisfaction/response → Support

**Outputs**
- Primary category + secondary category
- A short label for the problem (e.g., “after-hours missed calls”)

---

### **A — Analyze (or Act)**
Identify root causes and determine if the pain is worth solving.

**Root-cause prompts**
- “What causes that to happen?”
- “Who does this today, and what triggers it?”
- “What tool(s) are involved?”
- “What happens if it’s not done?”

**Look for patterns**
- Repetition (same task, same decision)
- Manual handoffs (person-to-person transitions)
- Single points of failure (only one person knows how)
- High-context work (requires memory, tribal knowledge)
- After-hours exposure (calls, leads, emergencies)

**Validate it’s a must-have**
- Frequency: How often?
- Impact: Time/money/risk?
- Urgency: What breaks if it continues?
- Ownership: Who feels it most?

**Outputs**
- Root cause statement (not just symptoms)
- Success criteria (what “better” looks like)
- Candidate automations + expected ROI

---

### **N — Notify (or Note / Note Impact)**
Document findings and communicate the next steps.

**What to document**
- Problem summary (1–2 sentences)
- Category (Financial/Productivity/Process/Support)
- Evidence (quotes, examples, frequency)
- Impact estimate (time, cost, missed revenue, risk)
- Proposed automation approach (high-level)
- Next action (meeting scheduled, follow-up email, proposal)

**Where to store it**
- CRM record (structured fields + notes)
- Call history log (summary + key extracted fields)
- Internal doc/proposal outline

**Outputs**
- A clear, actionable problem brief
- Agreement on priorities and next steps

---

## SCAN in One Page (Quick Cheat Sheet)

- **S**: Gather the story + evidence of friction
- **C**: Assign the pain to a category
- **A**: Find root cause + validate it’s worth solving
- **N**: Document + communicate next actions

---

## Example (How It Looks in Practice)

**Pain:** “We miss calls after 5pm and lose leads.”
- **S:** Daily missed calls, no one answers after hours
- **C:** Support + Financial
- **A:** Root cause = no after-hours intake + manual callback process; impact = lost leads + reputation
- **N:** Recommend voice agent for intake + scheduling + CRM logging; schedule discovery call

## Best Fit for AI + Voice Automation
SCAN pain points map naturally to automations like:
- Voice agent intake and qualification
- After-hours call handling
- Appointment scheduling + confirmations
- Follow-up workflows (email/SMS)
- Data entry into CRM (Google Sheets)
- Routing/escalation based on urgency
