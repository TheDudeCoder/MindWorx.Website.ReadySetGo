Introducing ReadySetGo

Book more
appointments.

ReadySetGo is an AI booking assistant for service businesses. It follows up with new leads, answers booking calls, checks your live Google or Outlook calendar, schedules appointments, and sends personalized confirmations automatically.

No more chasing callbacks. No more endless scheduling back-and-forth. Just faster follow-up, cleaner booking, and more visibility into what happened.

Free. No Card. No Catch.

The Problem

Missed calls cost money.

Most service businesses don't lose leads because they're bad at what they do. They lose leads because they're busy.

A customer fills out a form after hours. The phone rings while your team is on a job. A lead asks for a time that's already booked. A simple appointment turns into four rounds of back-and-forth.

ReadySetGo handles the first response, the scheduling conversation, the booking, and the confirmation, automatically.

Your customers aren't picky.
They just want a fast, clear answer when they reach out.
How It Works

Five steps. Mostly hands-off.

From the moment a lead reaches out to the moment they're booked and confirmed, ReadySetGo runs the whole conversation for you.

STEP 01

Capture the request

Add a simple form to your website so customers can request service and share their details.

STEP 02

Follow up quickly

ReadySetGo can place an AI voice call within minutes, while the lead is still interested.

STEP 03

Check real availability

The assistant reads your live Google or Outlook calendar, respects your hours, and avoids conflicts.

STEP 04

Book the appointment

If the requested time is open, it books. If not, it offers nearby alternatives instead of losing the lead.

STEP 05

Confirm and inform

Customers get a personalized confirmation email with secure links to cancel or reschedule.

What's Included

Everything you need to book more and chase less.

AI Voice Booking Assistant

Handles outbound follow-up and inbound booking calls so customers can book, cancel, or reschedule by voice.

Website Lead Capture

Collect new requests from your existing site with an embedded form, iframe, or hosted booking page.

Google & Outlook Calendar

Books directly against the calendar your team already uses. One source of truth, no double-bookings.

Real-Time Availability

Offers slots based on your actual schedule, hours, appointment length, and configured buffers.

Alternative Time Suggestions

If the requested slot is taken, ReadySetGo offers nearby times to keep the conversation moving.

Personalized Confirmation Emails

Confirmation, cancellation, and reschedule emails that match your business tone and include the actual details.

Secure Cancel & Reschedule Links

Customers manage changes from secure links in their confirmation, no account or callback required.

Dashboard Visibility

Review contacts, appointments, calls, summaries, emails, and plan usage from one screen.
